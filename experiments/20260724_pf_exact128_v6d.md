## Deterministic repair

Exact128 v6d reuses Clean128 v5d's SHA-256 per-well/per-branch positive
31-bit seeds for the two `_pf_ancc` and two `_pf_z` implementations. Both
`_resamp` helpers are deterministic and receive parent-owned draws. The
selector's explicit `default_rng(seed)` and the learned 128-seed loop's
`np.random.seed(seed_base + s)` remain unchanged.

The namespace covers eight stage/split branches:

- `sp45_train_ancc`, `sp45_train_z`;
- `sp45_test_ancc`, `sp45_test_z`;
- `learned_train_ancc`, `learned_train_z`;
- `learned_test_ancc`, `learned_test_z`.
