## Runtime engineering

The exact public normal run reached the clean blend at about 627 seconds for
three visible wells. Its measured selector section used roughly 177 seconds and
the learned section roughly 67 seconds for those wells, with about 376 seconds
of setup/warm-up work. Extrapolating the PF-heavy per-well work to the expected
hidden scale and allowing scheduling variance gives an engineering estimate of
about **5.0–6.8 hours**.

The hard budget is 7.5 hours, with 30 minutes reserved for final output/audit
and two hours reserved before starting the learned stage. If the selector
reaches that boundary, remaining wells receive a last-visible-TVT fallback. If
the learned stage cannot start or fails, the projected SP45 submission is
restored and still passes through the fixed blend and dynamic contract.
